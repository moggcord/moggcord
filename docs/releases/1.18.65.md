# Moggcord 1.18.65

[Documentation](../README.md) / [Release notes](README.md) / 1.18.65

## Installer

- Restart Discord after installing, repairing or uninstalling again. Discord's new updater starts through its own launcher, so the installer now uses that launcher and only falls back to Update.exe on older installations.

## Downloads

Update from Moggcord settings in Discord or download the installer for your system. The macOS and Linux packages are preview builds. Technical assets and checksums are published under [updates-v1.18.65](https://github.com/moggcord/moggcord/releases/tag/updates-v1.18.65).
